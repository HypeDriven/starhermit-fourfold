How to play

Players take turns dropping a disc into a column. Each disc falls to the lowest free cell. The first player with four of their own discs in a straight line — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — wins. If every cell fills with no line, it is a draw.

Controls
  • Click or tap a column to drop.
  • ← → move between columns, Enter or Space drops.
  • 1–9 drop straight into that column number.
  • U undo, H hint, R restart, P or Esc pause.
Scoring

A win is worth 1000. Winning under the stage's par drops adds 25 per drop saved, and beating a par time adds 5 per second saved. A draw is worth 300.
